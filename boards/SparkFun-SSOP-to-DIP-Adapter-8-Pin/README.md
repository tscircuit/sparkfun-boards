# SparkFun SSOP-to-DIP Adapter 8-Pin

For more information about this board, visit the official SparkFun product page:

[SparkFun SSOP-to-DIP Adapter 8-Pin](https://www.sparkfun.com/sparkfun-ssop-to-dip-adapter-8-pin.html)

The footprint dimensions and hole positions follow SparkFun's [official Eagle board](https://github.com/sparkfun/SSOP-DIP_Adapter_8-Pin/blob/master/Hardware/SparkFun_adapter-ssop8-v10.brd): a 15.24 × 10.16 mm PCB, 12.7 mm between DIP rows, 2.54 mm hole pitch, 1.016 mm drills, and 1.8796 mm copper pads. The SSOP lands use 0.635 mm pitch and 2.2 × 0.35 mm pads with row centers at ±3.2 mm.

The adapter is supplied as a bare PCB. Both header rows and the SSOP footprint are marked `doNotPlace`; their holes, pads, and connections remain on the board.
